# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees who incur
business-related expenses.

## Eligible Expenses

The following categories are reimbursable:

- Travel (airfare, train, taxi, mileage at USD 0.67 per mile)
- Lodging (up to USD 250 per night in standard cities, USD 400 in tier-1 cities)
- Meals (up to USD 75 per day, receipts required above USD 25)
- Client entertainment (pre-approval required above USD 200)
- Home office equipment (up to USD 500 per year, manager approval required)

## Ineligible Expenses

- Personal entertainment
- Alcohol (unless part of an approved client event)
- Traffic fines and parking tickets
- First-class airfare without VP approval
- Spouse or partner travel

## Submission Process

1. Log into the Finance Portal and open "New Expense Report".
2. Attach itemized receipts for every line above USD 25.
3. Submit within 30 days of the expense date.
4. Reports submitted after 90 days will be rejected.

## Approval and Payment

Managers approve reports within 5 business days. Approved reports are paid with
the next payroll cycle, typically on the 15th or the last business day of the month.

## Corporate Card

Employees with a corporate card must reconcile transactions monthly. Unreconciled
transactions older than 60 days may result in card suspension.
