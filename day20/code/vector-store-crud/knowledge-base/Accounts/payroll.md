# Payroll Calendar and Guidelines

## Pay Cycle

Employees are paid semi-monthly:

- First payment: 15th of each month
- Second payment: last business day of each month

If a payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the
preceding business day.

## Timesheet Deadlines

- Timesheets for the 1st–15th period are due by the 18th.
- Timesheets for the 16th–end period are due by the 3rd of the following month.
  Late timesheets are processed in the next cycle.

## Deductions

Standard deductions include:

- Federal and state income tax
- Social security and Medicare
- Health, dental, and vision insurance premiums
- Retirement contributions (401k) as elected by the employee

## Overtime

Non-exempt employees are paid 1.5x their regular rate for hours worked beyond
40 in a workweek. Overtime must be pre-approved by the employee's manager.

## Bonuses

Annual performance bonuses are paid in the second pay cycle of March.
Sign-on bonuses are paid in the first pay cycle after the start date.

## Direct Deposit

Direct deposit is mandatory. New employees must submit banking details within
the first 3 days of employment. Paper checks are issued only in exceptional
cases and require CFO approval.

## Payslips

Payslips are available in the employee self-service portal on payday. Historical
payslips are retained for 7 years.
