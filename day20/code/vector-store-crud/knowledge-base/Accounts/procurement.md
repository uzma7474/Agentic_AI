# Procurement Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how goods and services are purchased on behalf of the company.

## Purchase Requisition

All purchases above USD 500 require a purchase requisition submitted through the
Finance Portal. Requisitions must include:

- Description of goods or services
- Estimated cost
- Business justification
- Cost center to be charged

## Purchase Orders

A Purchase Order (PO) is generated once a requisition is approved. Vendors must
reference the PO number on all invoices. Purchases made without a PO may not be
reimbursed.

## Approval Thresholds

- Up to USD 500: manager approval
- USD 501 – USD 5,000: director approval
- USD 5,001 – USD 25,000: VP approval
- USD 25,001 – USD 100,000: CFO approval
- Above USD 100,000: CEO approval

## Preferred Vendors

The company maintains a list of preferred vendors with pre-negotiated pricing.
Buyers should check the preferred vendor list before sourcing new suppliers.

## Sole Source Purchases

When only one vendor can supply an item, a Sole Source Justification form must
be attached to the requisition. This form requires VP approval regardless of amount.

## Receiving

Goods must be received in the portal within 3 business days of delivery. Three-way
match (PO, receipt, invoice) is required before payment.
