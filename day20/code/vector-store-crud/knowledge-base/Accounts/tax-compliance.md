# Tax Compliance Guidelines

## Sales Tax

The company collects sales tax in jurisdictions where it has nexus. The current
nexus states are California, New York, Texas, and Washington.

Sales tax returns are filed monthly by the 20th for the prior month.

## VAT (International)

For EU customers, VAT is charged at the destination rate. VAT returns are filed
quarterly through the One-Stop-Shop (OSS) mechanism.

## Withholding Tax

Payments to foreign vendors may be subject to withholding tax. The rate depends
on the vendor's country and whether a tax treaty applies. The Accounts team
maintains a withholding tax matrix updated annually.

## 1099 Reporting (US)

Contractors paid more than USD 600 in a calendar year must receive a Form 1099-NEC.
Contractors must submit a completed W-9 before their first payment.

## Corporate Income Tax

Quarterly estimated payments are due on April 15, June 15, September 15, and
January 15. The Finance team coordinates with external tax advisors.

## Record Retention

All tax-related records must be retained for at least 7 years.

## Audit Support

In the event of a tax audit, contact tax@example.com immediately. Do not respond
directly to tax authorities without consulting the Finance team.
