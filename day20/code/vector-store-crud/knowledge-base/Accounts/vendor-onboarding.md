# Vendor Onboarding Process

## Overview

All new vendors must be onboarded before any invoice can be processed.

## Required Documents

Vendors must provide:

- Completed W-9 (US) or W-8BEN (non-US)
- Certificate of insurance (if services are performed on-site)
- Bank account verification letter
- Signed Master Services Agreement (MSA)

## Steps

1. Vendor submits the onboarding form at https://finance.example.com/vendors.
2. Accounts reviews documents within 5 business days.
3. Compliance performs a sanctions and watchlist screening.
4. Vendor is added to the vendor master record.
5. A welcome email with the vendor ID is sent.

## Vendor Master Record

The vendor master record stores legal name, tax identifier, bank details, payment
terms, and primary contact. Changes to bank details require re-verification via
a callback to a known phone number.

## Ongoing Requirements

- Vendors must renew insurance certificates annually.
- Vendors must notify Accounts of any change in legal name or ownership.
- Inactive vendors (no activity for 24 months) are archived.

## Blocked Vendors

Vendors appearing on government sanctions lists are blocked automatically. Any
attempt to onboard a blocked vendor is escalated to the Compliance Officer.
