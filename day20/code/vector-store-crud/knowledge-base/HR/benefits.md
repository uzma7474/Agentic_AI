# Employee Benefits Overview

## Eligibility

Full-time employees are eligible for benefits from the first day of employment.
Part-time employees working 20+ hours per week are eligible for medical only.

## Health Insurance

Three medical plans are offered:

- Basic PPO
- Standard PPO
- High-deductible plan with HSA

The company covers 80% of employee premiums and 50% of dependent premiums.

## Dental and Vision

Dental and vision coverage are provided at no cost to the employee.
Dependents may be added at the employee's expense.

## Retirement

The company offers a 401(k) plan with a 4% employer match, vested immediately.
Enrollment is available from the first day of employment.

## Life and Disability Insurance

- Basic life insurance: 2x annual salary, company-paid
- Short-term disability: 60% of salary, up to 12 weeks
- Long-term disability: 60% of salary, after 90 days

## Wellness

- Annual wellness stipend of USD 500
- Free access to the on-site gym
- Employee assistance program (EAP) with 6 free counseling sessions per year

## Additional Perks

- Commuter benefits (pre-tax)
- Tuition reimbursement up to USD 5,000 per year
- Employee referral bonus of USD 2,000 per successful hire

## Enrollment

New hires must enroll within 30 days of their start date. Outside of the annual
open enrollment window, changes are only permitted for qualifying life events.
