# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and
retaliation. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and interns.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat colleagues with respect and professionalism
- Communicate honestly and constructively
- Protect confidential company information
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations

## Prohibited Behavior

- Harassment of any kind, including sexual harassment
- Discrimination based on race, gender, religion, age, disability, or
  any protected characteristic
- Bullying, intimidation, or threats
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith
- Theft, fraud, or misuse of company assets

## Confidentiality

Employees must not disclose confidential information during or after employment.
This includes customer data, financial results, and trade secrets.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any outside activity that could conflict with their
duties. Disclosure is made through the HR portal.

## Reporting Concerns

Concerns may be reported to:

- Your manager
- HR at hr@example.com
- The anonymous ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0199

All reports are handled confidentially. Retaliation is strictly prohibited.

## Consequences

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## Contact

Ethics questions: ethics@example.com.
