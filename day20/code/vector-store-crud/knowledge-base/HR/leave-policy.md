# Leave and Time-Off Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes the types of leave available to employees, how to request
leave, and how approvals are handled.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid annual leave per month, totaling
21 days per year. Accrual begins on the first day of employment.

Unused annual leave may be carried over up to 5 days into the next calendar year.
Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Sick leave may be used for:

- Personal illness or injury
- Medical appointments
- Care of an immediate family member

For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note is required.

## Parental Leave

- Birth parents: 16 weeks of paid leave
- Non-birth parents: 8 weeks of paid leave
- Adoption: 8 weeks of paid leave

Parental leave must be requested at least 30 days in advance where possible.

## Bereavement Leave

Up to 5 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member.
Up to 2 paid days for extended family.

## Public Holidays

The company observes 10 public holidays per year. The list is published every
January on the HR portal.

## How to Request Leave

1. Log into the HR portal.
2. Select "Request Time Off".
3. Choose the leave type and dates.
4. Submit for manager approval.

Requests should be submitted at least 5 business days in advance for annual
leave, and as soon as possible for sick leave.
