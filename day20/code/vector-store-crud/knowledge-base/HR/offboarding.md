# Employee Offboarding Process

## Notice Period

- Individual contributors: 2 weeks
- Managers: 4 weeks
- Directors and above: 8 weeks

Notice must be submitted in writing to the manager and HR.

## Exit Interview

All departing employees are invited to an exit interview with HR. Participation
is voluntary. Feedback is aggregated and shared with leadership quarterly.

## Knowledge Transfer

The departing employee must:

- Document ongoing projects
- Hand over access credentials
- Introduce successors to key stakeholders
- Complete a knowledge transfer checklist signed by the manager

## Equipment Return

All company equipment must be returned on or before the last working day:

- Laptop and charger
- Badge and access cards
- Mobile phone (if provided)
- Any other company property

## Final Paycheck

The final paycheck includes:

- Salary through the last working day
- Accrued but unused annual leave (up to 5 days)
- Any approved but unpaid expenses

Final pay is issued on the next regular payday, or sooner where required by law.

## Benefits Termination

- Health insurance ends on the last day of the month of termination
- COBRA information is mailed within 14 days
- 401(k) remains vested; no action required

## Access Revocation

IT disables email and system access at end of business on the last working day.
Managers must submit the offboarding request at least 3 days in advance.
