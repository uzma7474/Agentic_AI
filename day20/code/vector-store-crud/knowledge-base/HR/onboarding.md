# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Day One

The hiring manager must:

- Confirm the start date with HR
- Ensure a laptop and access badges are requested
- Assign an onboarding buddy from the team

The new employee will receive a welcome email with:

- First-day instructions
- Login credentials for the HR portal
- A checklist of documents to bring

## Day One Checklist

- Complete the I-9 and W-4 forms
- Submit a government-issued photo ID
- Sign the employee handbook acknowledgment
- Set up company email and SSO
- Meet the onboarding buddy and team

## First Week

- Complete mandatory compliance training (security, anti-harassment)
- Attend a benefits orientation session
- Review the 30/60/90 day plan with the manager
- Set up direct deposit in the payroll portal

## First 30 Days

- Complete all role-specific training
- Establish weekly 1:1 meetings with the manager
- Submit the first timesheet

## Probation Period

New employees are on probation for the first 90 days. During this period,
either party may terminate employment with 1 week notice.

## Equipment

Laptops are company property and must be returned on the last working day.
Personal use is permitted within the acceptable-use policy.
